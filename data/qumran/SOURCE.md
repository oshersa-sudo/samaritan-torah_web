# Qumran (Dead Sea Scrolls) biblical text — source & attribution

`verses.jsonl` holds the Pentateuch text of the Qumran biblical scrolls, one row per verse
(best-preserved scroll), extracted and aligned by `scripts/qumran/build.py` and stored in
`verses.qumran_text` / `verses.qumran_scroll`.

**Source:** the [ETCBC/dss](https://github.com/ETCBC/dss) Text-Fabric dataset.
**Transcriptions & morphology:** Martin G. Abegg, Jr., James E. Bowley, and Edward M. Cook.
**Text-Fabric conversion:** ETCBC — Jarod Jacobs, Martijn Naaijer, Dirk Roorda.
**License:** Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 (CC-BY-NC 4.0).

This app is a non-commercial, voluntary project; the attribution above is shown in-app
(the ⓘ info panel of the Qumran comparison). Reconstructed portions are included; damage/
uncertainty flags were stripped for readability, and each verse shows one scroll witness.
